**The Sound of Leather on Willow**

Under a sky brushed wide with gold,
Where stories of heroes are forever told,
A quiet pitch in the morning light
Awaits the battle, the glorious fight.

Boots press dreams into the ground,
Heartbeat syncing with each small sound,
A red ball rests in a bowler’s hand,
Like thunder waiting at command.

He runs — the wind becomes his guide,
Power and purpose in every stride,
The seam cuts air, the crowd holds breath,
One moment dancing close to death.

Crack! goes willow, sharp and clean,
A flash of timing, pure and keen,
The ball now races, chased by hope,
Across the grass’s emerald slope.

Fielders dive like falling stars,
Leaving sweat and unseen scars,
While voices rise in waves that roll,
A roaring, living, single soul.

Spin then whispers ancient art,
Turning doubt in batter’s heart,
Footwork falters, eyes deceive,
The pitch has tricks up its sleeve.

Hours fade but time stands still,
Every run a test of will,
Sunburnt faces, dusty whites,
Battles fought in silent fights.

Last over. Silence, tight and deep.
Nerves no warrior learns to keep.
One swing, one edge, one diving save —
Between the brave and the more brave.

Then cheers explode, the sky takes flight,
Day dissolves into electric night,
For cricket is not just bat and ball —
It is a pulse that binds us all.
